Judge each pillar against these definitions.

Operational Excellence: running and monitoring the system well. Look for monitoring and alarms, central logging, infrastructure as code, automated deployment pipelines and runbooks.

Security: protecting data and systems. Look for network isolation (private subnets, security groups), edge protection (WAF), encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access and audit logging.

Reliability: recovering from failure and meeting demand. Look for multiple Availability Zones, no single points of failure, health checks, auto scaling, backups and a disaster recovery plan.

Performance Efficiency: using resources well as demand changes. Look for caching, a CDN, suitable compute and database choices, and scaling that follows load.

Cost Optimization: avoiding waste. Look for right-sized resources, scaling that follows demand, suitable pricing models (such as Savings Plans), storage tiering and managed services where they save effort.

Sustainability: reducing environmental impact. Look for high utilisation, right-sizing, managed or serverless services, efficient instance types and data lifecycle rules.

Scoring guide:
1 = major gaps or single points of failure.
2 = significant gaps.
3 = a reasonable foundation with notable gaps.
4 = good practice with minor gaps.
5 = strong practice, clearly shown in the diagram.
